US Marriage Duration Data by Geography

Marriage duration and marital-status statistics (median/mean years married, marriage & divorce rates, percent currently married) across the United States, broken down by region, state, and metro area / county, with representative ZIP code ranges.

About this data and its granularity: No single free, bulk, nationwide dataset publishes "how long married couples stay together" tabulated down to individual ZIP codes. The Census Bureau's American Community Survey publishes marital-status / "years married" tables only down to the county or Public Use Microdata Area (PUMA) level in open tables, and the live Census and CDC/NCHS APIs are not reachable from the environment used to build this page (only GitHub, npm, and PyPI endpoints were reachable; no GitHub-hosted mirror of a ZIP-level marriage-duration dataset was found either). This page therefore reports the most granular tier available — metro area / county, with a representative ZIP range for that area — and the figures are representative estimates following published CDC NCHS and Census ACS state/metro reporting patterns (reference year 2021), not a verbatim download of an official table. Use the Source column as a starting point and consult CDC NCHS or data.census.gov directly for authoritative figures.
