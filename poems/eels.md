# Eels

Beneath the dock, where shadows pool,
A ribbon moves, dark, slow, and cool.
No fins to flash, no scales to gleam—
Just muscle writing through the stream.

They slither out from river mud,
From brackish creek and tidal flood,
And some, they say, will cross the sea
To spawn where ancient currents be.

A thousand miles with no map drawn,
They swim by night and hide by dawn,
Through Sargasso's weedy maze,
Born again in salt-dark days.

So raise a glass to the humble eel,
Electric, strange, and hard to feel—
A fish that bends where others break,
Half mystery and half a snake.
